These corrections remain subject to the complete editorial review and browser regression.

### Existing baseline content scan

A direct parse of the complete feature-branch `index.html` English array confirmed the following current baseline profile:

- 810 entries; all 810 have a headword, part of speech, pronunciation, English definition, Simplified Chinese definition, examples array and tip.
- 126 entries currently have fewer than two general example sentences (down from 258 after integrating 132 second-example patches).
- 240 entries contain a `senses` array, comprising 526 senses; all 526 have a Simplified Chinese sense translation (up from 100 entries / 244 senses after integrating 140 multi-POS sense patches).
- 570 entries do not have an explicit `senses` array. All 140 baseline entries with multiple parts of speech and no prior sense array have now received sense-level records; the remaining 570 are primarily single-POS entries and still require review for conflated meanings.
- 38 entries lack the optional `breakdown` and `related` fields.
- None of the 810 baseline entries currently has category assignments; Phase 3 must classify and QA the existing entries as well as the additions.
- All 810 baseline pronunciations passed the simple slash-delimited notation check. This does not validate phonetic accuracy or accent consistency.

This scan is a structural inventory, not a linguistic accuracy certification. The 216 short/missing example sets and the 680 entries without explicit sense structures are the remaining review queues.

These are structural/content-shape checks. They are not a substitute for the full linguistic review, native-quality translation review, runtime validation, or the final audit of all 810 existing entries.
