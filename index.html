Delete Account

Permanently delete your SwiftDictionary account and its cloud-synchronised data, including saved words, profile, preferences and learning records. This does not delete your Google account. Choose this option only when you want to close the account permanently; deleting learning history alone is a separate action.

Other devices will lose refresh access immediately; an access token already issued to another device can remain valid until it expires.

Learning history

Delete learning history only: lesson progress, practice summaries, Daily Learning sessions, Flashcard game history and Flashcard mastery. Your SwiftDictionary account, saved words, Flashcard collections, custom cards, profile and preferences will remain. Completions already used today will still count until the normal allowance reset. To permanently close your account and delete its associated cloud data, use the separate Delete account option above.

If your last sign-in was more than 15 minutes ago, sign out and sign in again before requesting deletion.
