Free vs Premium

SwiftDictionary keeps dictionary lookup open to everyone. The Premium plan is designed to offer more room for daily learning and organising saved vocabulary.

ⓘ
Premium is not currently available to purchase. This page is an informational comparison of configured learning allowances and active plan-based folder limits. There is no checkout, subscription or payment service yet.

How Daily Learning allowances workEach of the four categories has its own daily allowance, shared across English and Chinese. Completing an activity successfully uses one allowance. Starting, resuming or abandoning an activity does not use a completion allowance.
Permanent lesson flexibilityThe lesson allowance applies to starting a new permanent lesson. Resuming an unfinished lesson and reopening a completed lesson for review remain free and do not consume another start.
Local-time resetDaily limits reset at midnight in the timezone selected in the learner’s preferences. Changing between English and Chinese does not create a second allowance.
Collections folder limitsFolder creation is limited to 16 for Free accounts and 40 for Premium accounts. Existing folders are preserved if an account plan changes; folders are never automatically deleted.

SwiftDictionary is an independent vocabulary-learning project. Premium is not currently available to purchase. Folder limits follow the plan assigned to the account.
