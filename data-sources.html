Dictionary Data Sources

This page identifies third-party data used to support the Chinese single-character dictionary. Individual records may combine source reference data with SwiftDictionary-authored categories, examples, explanations and learning notes. The page is an attribution notice, not a legal compliance certification.

CC-CEDICT Chinese–English dictionary

Selected single-character entries and adapted English glosses are based on CC-CEDICT, a community-maintained Chinese–English dictionary originally made available through MDBG. SwiftDictionary has selected, curated and modified entries and adapted some glosses into concise Simplified Chinese explanations. This content is not endorsed by CC-CEDICT or MDBG.

  • Source: CC-CEDICT · MDBG project page
  • Snapshot: 20 November 2026; mirror commit 9f027e342cc57310780f4db0355f54f90d33674d
  • Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)

CC-CEDICT-derived dictionary data and adapted glosses are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Chinese Dictionary remains free and unrestricted; no additional access restrictions are imposed on this licensed data.

U2.8.0 Chinese Flashcards: the multi-character vocabulary draft includes 904 entries with CC-CEDICT-derived English glosses and pronunciation references from the same 20 November 2026 snapshot. Those derived fields remain under CC BY-SA 4.0. The separate 24 useful-expression cards, learning cues and contextual examples are SwiftDictionary-authored unless a record identifies another source.

Unicode Unihan 18.0.0

Mandarin pronunciation reference data is based on the Unicode Unihan Database, including the kMandarin, kHanyuPinyin and kHanyuPinlu properties. Readings may vary by word and context, so the data is checked against common usage.

  • Data: Unicode 18.0.0 Unihan data
  • Property documentation: Unicode Standard Annex #38
  • Licence: Unicode License v3
  • Extracted pronunciation files: mozillazg/pinyin-data, Unicode 18.0.0 snapshot (kMandarin, kHanyuPinyin and kHanyuPinlu); extraction repository licence: MIT, Copyright © 2016 mozillazg.
